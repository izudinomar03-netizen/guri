01

Executive summary

GURI HOTEL is a mid-to-upscale, full-service hospitality company combining accommodation (25 rooms in 5 categories), a restaurant (GURI Kitchen, about 90 seats across the main hall, family section, rooftop and a VIP majlis) and events (conference and wedding hall). It targets business travellers, NGO and government delegations, visiting diaspora families and local diners who want safety, reliability and real Somali hospitality.

  • Seasonality: peaks in June–August (diaspora) and around Eid. Ramadan shifts the restaurant to iftar and suhoor packages.
  • Competition: established beach-area and airport-zone hotels. GURI competes on consistency (24/7 power, clean rooms, fast service), family friendliness, and easy payment via mobile money.
  • Advantage: mobile-money payments (EVC Plus, Zaad, Sahal) built into the POS from day one, plus direct online and WhatsApp booking.

06

Marketing & sales

  • Direct booking first: website booking form (already live on the ERP site), WhatsApp Business, phone. Offer a direct-booking perk (free welcome tea and coffee, airport pick-up discount).
  • Social: TikTok and Facebook content (food, rooftop sunsets, weddings) plus Instagram, all with a Google Business Profile and Maps listing.
  • Corporate: negotiated annual rates for NGOs, ministries and companies, with monthly invoicing via ERPNext.
  • Diaspora: partnerships with travel agents in Minneapolis, London, Nairobi and Dubai, plus summer family packages.
  • OTAs: Booking.com and Expedia for international reach, capped at about 20% of rooms to control commission.
  • Restaurant: iftar buffets in Ramadan, Friday family lunch, wedding catering packages.
